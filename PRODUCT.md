# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript

## Users

University students preparing for major exams in a focused, distraction-free environment.

## Product Purpose

An intelligent, focused operating system for studying that prioritizes deep work and data-driven progress tracking without the noise of generic SaaS apps.

## Operating Context

Students using the application during intense study sessions, requiring high focus, minimal distractions, and clear visibility into their study progress and knowledge gaps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Must preserve the visual rules in DESIGN.md (calm, focused, premium, no excessive gradients/glassmorphism), the motion constraints (motion/react), and the Next.js/Tailwind stack.

## Product Principles

1. **Focus First:** Eliminate distractions and visual noise to support deep cognitive work.
2. **Data-Driven Progress:** Provide clear, actionable insights into study performance and gaps.
3. **Calm Aesthetics:** Use a premium, structured, and muted design language that feels academic and trustworthy.
